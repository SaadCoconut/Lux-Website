Data protection & ethics

Coconut Luxembourg complies with GDPR requirements and Coconutwork’s safeguarding framework. Participant data is stored securely, anonymised for reporting, and retained only for as long as required by funders.

Ethical review procedures ensure that all research, storytelling, and evaluation activities respect the dignity and agency of everyone involved.
