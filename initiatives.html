Initiatives & projects

From circular economy incubators to intercultural residencies, Coconut Luxembourg delivers 16 active projects and a suite of local initiatives rooted in our four priority areas.

Design Thinking & Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship labs supporting inclusive business models and social innovation.

Circular Start Lab

2025 · Luxembourg City

Entrepreneurship coaching for young innovators transforming waste into new products while honouring Luxembourg craft traditions.

CoLab Social Ventures

2024 · Esch-sur-Alzette

Multi-city incubator for social enterprises focusing on inclusive financing solutions and measurable impact.

Women in Tech Catalyst

2023 · Luxembourg City

Accelerator programme pairing young women in technology with mentors from five EU countries to fast-track their ventures.

Agro-Innovation Residency

2022 · Diekirch

Residency in the north of Luxembourg where youth entrepreneurs prototype digital agriculture and food resilience solutions.

Intercultural Dialogue & Peace-building

Cross-border dialogue that amplifies storytelling, empathy, and shared governance.

Mediatech Youth Exchange

2024 · Dudelange

Euro-Mediterranean exchange focused on media literacy and anti-racist narratives with delegations from eight countries.

Cross-Border Dialogue Series

2025 · Grevenmacher

Dialogue events between Luxembourg, Saarbrücken, and Metz advancing interreligious understanding and youth policy collaboration.

Heritage Storytellers

2022 · Wiltz

Immersive storytelling projects with museums that blend oral history, digital archives, and community exhibitions.

Mindful Cities Challenge

2024 · Luxembourg City

City lab integrating wellbeing and cultural initiatives to design more inclusive public spaces alongside young residents.

Digital Participation

Digital literacy, civic tech, and inclusive online participation for all communities.

Digital Bridges Hackathon

2025 · Esch-Belval

48-hour hackathon co-creating civic tech tools with youth delegations from Portugal, Morocco, and Luxembourg.

Digital Democracy Bootcamp

2023 · Clervaux

Bootcamp where young people test AI moderation and participation apps that support local Agenda 2030 processes.

Inclusive Makers Camp

2024 · Schifflange

STEM camp focused on accessibility, developing inclusive open-source tools for youth centres and libraries.

Youth Digital Ambassadors

2022 · Echternach

Programme training digital mentors who support seniors and newcomers with online skills and media literacy.

Environmental Sustainability

Climate resilience, biodiversity, and circular economy solutions led by young people.

Lux Green Labs

2025 · Differdange

Urban gardening and biodiversity labs co-created with high schools in Differdange and Bertrange to steward green spaces.

EcoMentors Fellowship

2023 · Ettelbruck

Year-long fellowship equipping youth workers as climate coaches who accompany schools through CO₂ audits.

Sustainable Schools Accelerator

2024 · Mersch

Municipal partnerships and energy transition roadmaps that embed sustainability across school communities.

Climate Resilience Forum

2025 · Remich

Annual forum on climate adaptation featuring study visits and peer learning across regional protection projects.

Local initiatives across Luxembourg

Community-driven programmes complement our flagship portfolio.

Youth Commons Ettelbruck

Pop-up lab for agricultural innovation connecting local producers with young researchers.

Digital Inclusion Bus

Travelling tech hub visiting each canton to deliver hands-on digital participation trainings.

Esch Youth Climate Cafés

Monthly dialogues on climate justice hosted at Kulturfabrik Esch.

North Meets South Dialogue

Exchange days bringing youth centres from the north and the Minett region together.

Community Impact Studio

Design sprint series helping local associations co-create new youth services.

Eco Trails Luxembourg

Guided nature walks with citizen science activities that inspire climate action.
