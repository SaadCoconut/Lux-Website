Coconut Luxembourg is the national branch of the Coconutwork network. We support youth-led innovation through entrepreneurship programmes, intercultural dialogue, digital participation, and environmental action.

About Coconut Luxembourg

We are a non-profit association (ASBL) advancing the Coconutwork vision in Luxembourg. Our team delivers non-formal education programmes that help young people build inclusive ventures, intercultural bridges, and greener futures across Europe and the Mediterranean region.

Read our story

Our areas of activity

Each programme combines design thinking, youth leadership, and strategic partnerships to respond to the needs of Luxembourg’s communities.

Design Thinking & Entrepreneurship

Incubators, accelerators, and mentoring that help young innovators test solutions, secure investment, and scale social impact ventures.

Intercultural Dialogue & Peace-building

Exchanges, dialogue labs, and cultural residencies that strengthen mutual understanding across Euro-Mediterranean communities.

Digital Participation

Civic tech bootcamps, media literacy programmes, and mentorship initiatives that ensure everyone has a voice in digital spaces.

Environmental Sustainability

Climate coaching, citizen science projects, and circular economy labs co-created with schools, municipalities, and youth organisations.
