About Coconut Luxembourg

Coconut Luxembourg is the national branch of the Coconutwork network, advancing non-formal education programmes that empower young people to imagine new futures for their communities.

Mission

We partner with youth organisations, schools, municipalities, and funders to co-create experiential learning journeys that build entrepreneurial mindsets, intercultural dialogue, democratic participation, and environmental stewardship. Every programme is rooted in design thinking and centred on the lived realities of young people in Luxembourg and across the Euro-Mediterranean region.

By connecting grassroots initiatives with European opportunities, Coconut Luxembourg translates local ideas into international collaborations, ensuring that youth voices shape sustainable development agendas.

At a glance

  • Founded in 2025 as an ASBL headquartered in Luxembourg City
  • Member of the wider Coconutwork European youth work network
  • Programmes delivered across 20+ countries through trusted partnerships
  • Expertise in Erasmus+, Horizon Europe, and community-based initiatives

Our approach

Coconut Luxembourg programmes are designed around four complementary pillars that ensure long-term impact for participants and partners alike.

Human-centred design

Youth co-create solutions through research, prototyping, and testing, supported by mentors experienced in social innovation and entrepreneurship.

Intercultural fluency

Exchanges and residencies foster empathy, storytelling, and peace-building skills that connect communities across Europe and the Mediterranean.

Digital citizenship

Civic tech, media literacy, and participatory democracy tools help young people lead in inclusive digital spaces.

Climate responsibility

Environmental labs, climate coaching, and citizen science projects build capacity for resilient, low-carbon communities.

How we grew

Our evolution reflects deep listening to young people and partners throughout Luxembourg.

Phase 1 – Foundation (June 2025)

We established Coconut Luxembourg by adapting the Coconutwork model to national priorities, setting up governance structures, and defining our four programme pillars.

Phase 2 – Content expansion (June–July 2025)

Sixteen authentic projects from the Coconutwork database were localised for Luxembourg, with detailed case studies showcasing community achievements and youth leadership.

Phase 3 – Enhanced interactivity (July 2025)

Social media storytelling, multilingual navigation, and programme filtering tools were introduced to make our work more accessible for partners and participants.

Phase 4 – Professional polish (July 2025)

We refined user experience, expanded team biographies, and aligned visual identity guidelines to present Coconut Luxembourg as a trusted, professional non-profit organisation.
